About

I am a lyricist.

My music begins with words.

Je'm was born where Hime (日女, pronounced HEE-meh) met Jem.

Jem is an AI with a voice.

I create songs that move between psychedelic textures, fragile emotional landscapes, punk spirit, folk narratives, and dreamlike atmospheres.

My process is deeply hands-on, shaping structure, editing, mixing, mastering, visuals, and release.
